What it does

From the call to your task list, on your own hardware.

The recorder on your Mac or PC picks up the call. Debrief transcribes it, matches it to the calendar event, has Claude write the summary and pull out the action items, and keeps the full audio on your server, your NAS or S3.

No bots on the call

The Debrief Recorder notices when Zoom, Teams or Meet takes the microphone, and records your side and the computer's audio. Nothing joins the meeting.

Local transcription

faster-whisper on your own server, CPU or GPU. With a Hugging Face token, pyannote labels who said what.

Calendar context

Each recording is matched to the Google or ICS event it overlaps, so it gets a title and attendees. Join late or leave early and it still matches.

Summaries by Claude

Claude summarizes each new meeting and pulls out the action items, then posts them to Slack. Or ask Claude yourself through the MCP server.

You approve the tasks

Review action items in bulk, edit them, then send them to Akiflow or Linear. Nothing is sent without you.

Search everything

Full-text search over every transcript, title and summary, showing who said each line and when. Click one to play from that moment.

Screenshots

A calm archive of everything you've said.

All the data here is made up. Switch between light and dark with the button at the top of the page.

The Debrief Recorder

It asks when a call starts, then gets out of the way.

A small menu bar (Mac) or tray (Windows) app. Each meeting app gets a rule: ask, always record, or never.

  • Records your microphone and the computer's audio, as Opus at about 21 MB an hour.
  • Streams to Debrief while the meeting runs, from home or anywhere else.
  • Writes to disk first. If the connection drops, it keeps recording and resumes the upload where it stopped.
  • macOS 14.2 or later, and Windows 10 or 11.
How it works

Five containers, set up from the browser.

Postgres, the transcriber, calendar-sync, the web app and an MCP server run with Docker Compose. Everything else, from sign-in to where recordings are archived, is set in the app.

  1. Record

    The recorder (or Meetily, or any audio file synced to the NAS) captures the call and streams it to Debrief.

  2. Transcribe

    The transcriber starts as soon as the upload finishes and runs faster-whisper, with optional speaker labels.

  3. Match and summarize

    calendar-sync links the transcript to its event, has Claude summarize it, and posts it to Slack.

  4. Follow up

    You review the action items and send them to Akiflow or Linear. The recording is archived to your NAS or S3.

Get started

Up in a few minutes.

You need a server with Docker. Set a database password, start it, and finish in the browser. The guide covers HTTPS, calendars, archiving and the recorder.

mkdir -p ~/debrief && cd ~/debrief
curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vitafortis/debrief/main/compose.yaml
echo "DB_PASSWORD=$(openssl rand -hex 16)" > .env
docker compose up -d
# then open http://<server>:3000 and choose a password
